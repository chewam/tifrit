## Seven years without rain

From 2018 to 2025, the valley went through the longest drought anyone remembers. The Paradise Valley pools emptied. The Imouzzer waterfalls fell silent. Gardens were abandoned. The house's spring held, only just.

## The water comes back

The rains of winter 2025-2026 recharged the aquifers. The springs started again, the pools filled up, the Imouzzer waterfalls are flowing. The flow is modest at the end of summer, it is beautiful from November to May. These photos are recent.

## What the house does

Facts, which you can check on site.

{{facts}}
### Spring water
The pool is filled by the spring, not the mains. The garden is drip-irrigated. Linen is changed on request, not daily.

### The sun
Hot water in the rooms comes from solar heaters on the roof. Lighting is LED.

### Rammed earth
Walls of soil from the land, built by the grandfather. Nothing to transport, nothing to air-condition in summer.

### Water in carafes
Filtered water in carafes at the table and in the rooms, solid soap in the bathrooms. Plastic stays in Agadir.

### The village producers
Honey, argan oil, vegetables and bread come from the valley. We take you to meet the people who make them.

### People
The whole team lives in the valley. So do the guides we recommend.
{{/facts}}

## Berber culture, in the present

People here speak Tashelhit, the Amazigh language of the Souss. The argan tree grows on the slopes and its oil is pressed by a women's cooperative in the region. Today's beekeepers make thyme and euphorbia honey. In Inzerki, an hour's drive away, the largest collective apiary in the world, made of earth and wood, is still in use. The river gardens are farmed in terraces, as you see them from the trail. These are the people of 2026, not a backdrop.

## Away from the crowds

The natural pools of Paradise Valley receive buses from Agadir around 11 am. From the house, you go early by the marked trail and leave when the others arrive. The other loop, to the oasis, sees almost nobody: three to four hours of walking, the palm grove, the limestone slope, the terraced gardens, tea with a local family.

## Walking without damage

The trails are recovering from the drought. We stay on the paths, leave the plants where they are, carry our waste back. Closed shoes: the rocks at the waterfalls are slippery. The valley road can be cut after a storm, we check the day before.

## The position and the view

The house sits at 640 m, above Paradise Valley, in the palm grove. Opposite, the limestone ridges. Below, the river and the village gardens. The marked trails start at the door.

In the evening, dinner is on the terrace above the palm grove. The table is shared, and open to anyone who wants to sit down. In winter, dinner is by the fireplace. In the afternoon, the spring-fed pool.

## Your days are your own

Every morning, an outing is offered. You leave at dawn with a guide from the village, or you stay by the water until evening. Breakfast waits for you whenever suits you. Dinner is ordered during the day. The Wi-Fi handles video calls: a morning of work on the terrace, then the valley.

## Rammed earth, the spring, the sun

The walls are rammed earth: soil from the land, compacted layer by layer. The grandfather built them. They are thick, cool in summer, and hold the warmth of the fireplace in winter. The pool is filled by the valley spring, not by the mains. Hot water in the rooms comes from solar heaters on the roof.

{{photo: A rammed-earth wall up close, morning light}}

## The six rooms

Six rooms, all with a view over the valley. Air conditioning, private bathroom, Wi-Fi, towels and daily housekeeping. Some have a fireplace and a private terrace. In the evening, the silence of the valley takes the place of television.

## The hosts

Rachid and his family run the house. The rammed-earth walls are the grandfather's. In the evening the table is shared, open to anyone who wants to sit down, and free for those who prefer their terrace. Tashelhit, Arabic and French are spoken here.

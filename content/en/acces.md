## Where we are

The house is on the Paradise Valley road (RP1001), between Aourir and Imouzzer Ida Outanane, in the palm grove at 640 m. The marker on the map is the entrance of the house.

## By plane

Direct three-hour flights to Agadir-Al Massira from Paris, Lyon and Nantes, and from many other European cities. Arrive in daylight if you can: the valley road is nicer before dark.

## From the airport or Agadir to the house

- **The house shuttle.** On request, to book with your stay. Rachid or a driver from the village waits for you at the airport or at your hotel in Agadir. Price on request.
- **By taxi.** Ask for "Tifrit, Paradise Valley, Imouzzer road". Agree on the price before leaving.
- **By rental car.** Rental companies are at the airport. From Agadir, take the Aourir road (N1 northbound), then at Aourir turn towards Imouzzer Ida Outanane. Pass the village of Alma, follow the "Paradise Valley" signs. The house is by the road, after the pools. Free parking in front of the house.
- **By bus.** Buses and shared taxis link Agadir to Imouzzer. Tell us your arrival time, we come and pick you up in Imouzzer or at the pools.

## Before you leave

- Bring cash: the last cash machine is in Aourir, at the entrance of the valley.
- Closed shoes for the trails, swimwear for the pools, a jacket for winter evenings.
- Check-in from 2 pm, check-out before noon. Tell us if you arrive late, we keep dinner for you.

## Connected, like at home

Wi-Fi covers the rooms, the lounge and the terrace, fast enough for video calls and streaming. Your family reaches you as usual, by internet call or message.

If you work remotely, it works here: a video meeting from the terrace in the morning, the trail in the afternoon. Several guests extend their stay for that. Tell us when you book, we keep you a room with a table and good light. Mobile coverage stops at the entrance of the valley: everything goes through the Wi-Fi.
